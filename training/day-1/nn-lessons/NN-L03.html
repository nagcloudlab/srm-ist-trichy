Can you choose the direction from the gradient?

01

What does a negative gradient mean?

Increasing w lowers loss — move w upward.
02

Positive gradient: increase or decrease w?

Decrease the weight — move opposite the gradient.
03

What does a zero gradient mean?

Loss is locally flat — no directional update needed.
04

Does the gradient tell us how far to move?

No — it gives direction only. Step size comes from the learning rate.
