Learning is repeated correction

  • ✓ Training repeats loss, gradient, and update calculations.
  • ✓ The gradient must be refreshed at every new weight.
  • ✓ Updates shrink as the model approaches the minimum.
  • ✓ A useful learning rate converges; an oversized one diverges.
  • ✓ After ten stable steps, the neuron predicts 4 km in about 13 minutes.
