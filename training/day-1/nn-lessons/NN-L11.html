🧠 Knowledge Check

01 — At each connection, what do we multiply the blame by?

Click to reveal

The local derivative of that connection.

02 — If z = 5, does ReLU pass the blame?

Click to reveal

Yes — z is positive, gate is OPEN, blame passes through.

03 — If z = −3, does ReLU pass the blame?

Click to reveal

No — z is negative, gate is CLOSED, blame is blocked (gradient = 0).

04 — Why save z during the forward pass?

Click to reveal

The backward pass needs z to decide if the ReLU gate was open or closed.

05 — Why update all weights AFTER computing all gradients?

Click to reveal

All gradients must describe the same parameter state.
