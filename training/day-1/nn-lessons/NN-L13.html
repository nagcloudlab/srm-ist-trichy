Sigmoid plus BCE gives a simple gradient

BLAME AT THE SCORE
gradient = p − y
prediction probability minus target label

No long symbolic derivation is needed to train this output neuron.

p − y says which way the score should move

TARGET 1 • PREDICTED 0.8
0.8 − 1 = −0.2

negative gradient → push score up

TARGET 0 • PREDICTED 0.8
0.8 − 0 = +0.8

positive gradient → push score down

Average the blame, then update w and b

01
Forward

p = sigmoid(w × x + b)

02
Error

each example: p − y

03
Average

grad_w = mean((p − y) × x)  •  grad_b = mean(p − y)

04
Update

w ← w − 0.1 × grad_w  •  b ← b − 0.1 × grad_b

One makes the boundary sharper

BATCH GRADIENTS

grad_b = 0.0000

w = 0.5 − 0.1 × (−0.4876)
w = 0.5488
b stays 0

The symmetric data cancels the bias gradient. The weight grows.
