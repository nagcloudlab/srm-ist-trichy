Learning rate turns direction into distance

  • ✓ The gradient supplies the direction.
  • ✓ The learning rate η scales the step size.
  • ✓ The update rule is wnew = wold − ηg.
  • ✓ Small rates learn slowly; large rates can overshoot.
  • ✓ Always verify that the new loss went down.
