Lesson 15

StyleGAN

The architecture that changed everything

These People Don't Exist

Every face on thispersondoesnotexist.com is generated by a StyleGAN-family model.

1024 x 1024 pixels. Photorealistic. No real person was photographed.

How? By rethinking the Generator from scratch.

StyleGAN didn't just add more layers. It changed how the Generator uses noise.

The Problem with Normal Generators

DCGAN Generator

z = [0.3, -0.7, 1.2, 0.5, ...]

What does z[0] control?

  • A little bit of hair color
  • A little bit of face shape
  • A little bit of background
  • A little bit of everything

Everything is entangled.

The Problem

z controls:

  • Face shape (high-level)
  • Eye color (mid-level)
  • Freckles, hair strands (fine detail)

All tangled in one vector.

You can't change JUST the hair color.

StyleGAN's Big Idea

Disentangle these controls.

The Mapping Network: z → w

Z-space (input)

Random, normally distributed. Must fill a sphere. Features forced to entangle.

Like cramming all clothes into a ball.

W-space (learned)

No distribution constraint. Can spread into whatever shape the data needs. Features separate naturally.

Like laying clothes in organized drawers.

Sizes: StyleGAN z, w ∈ R512 · our MNIST labs z = 64 · Unit 1’s DCGAN z = 100.

AdaIN: How Style Controls the Image

Adaptive Instance Normalization

1. Normalize x (instance norm)
   x_norm = (x - mean) / std

2. Get scale + shift from w
   gamma, beta = Linear(w)

3. Apply new style
   out = gamma * x_norm + beta

Step 1: Wash the canvas

Instance norm strips the current style from the feature map.

Step 2: Paint with new style

Scale (gamma) and shift (beta) from w inject the desired style.

w is injected at every layer via AdaIN. Each layer learns its own Linear(w → gamma, beta).

What Controls What?

Style Mixing

Result

A's face shape with B's skin/hair/color!

DCGAN: Impossible

z enters once at start. Can't separate face shape from hair color. Everything is entangled.

Style Mixing as Regularization

During training, randomly mix two w vectors. Forces the network to keep styles independent at each layer.

Noise Injection: Stochastic Details

Per-Layer Noise

x = Conv(previous_layer)
x = x + B * noise
x = AdaIN(x, w)

noise = random tensor (same spatial size)
B = learned per-channel scaling factor

Style (from w via AdaIN)

Controls WHAT the image looks like.

"This person has curly hair"

Noise (random per-pixel)

Controls exact random PLACEMENT.

"Exactly where each curl falls"

Truncation Trick

w′ = w̄ + ψ · (w − w̄)
psiEffect
1.0No truncation (full diversity, some weird faces)
0.7Mild (high quality, good diversity)
0.5Strong (very high quality, less diversity)
0.0Always the "average face"

In Z-space (L11)

You met this in L11: re-sample any zi with |zi| > t (BigGAN). Works — but z is entangled, so shrinking z changes many features unevenly.

In W-space

Pull w toward the average w̄. W is more disentangled, so this smoothly shrinks every style toward the average face → cleaner quality-vs-variety control.

StyleGAN Evolution

StyleGAN v1 (2018/19)

  • Mapping network + AdaIN
  • Progressive growing
  • Truncation trick
  • First photorealistic faces

StyleGAN2 (2020)

  • Dropped progressive growing
  • Replaced AdaIN with weight demodulation (fixed blob artifacts)
  • Skip connections + residual; path-length regularization
  • Cleaner images

StyleGAN3 (2021)

  • Fixed position-dependent artifacts
  • Anti-aliased layers
  • Objects move naturally in latent space
  • Alias-free generation

Why StyleGAN Matters Beyond Faces

Ideas That Spread Everywhere

  • Mapping networks: transform z before using it (widely reused)
  • Multi-scale injection: coarse/medium/fine control
  • W-space: a learned, more disentangled latent — the basis of GAN inversion and editing
  • Proof: GANs can be photorealistic

Applications Beyond Faces

  • Cars, rooms, cats, art
  • GAN inversion: find w for real image → edit it
  • Domain adaptation: face GAN → cartoon GAN
  • Medical imaging, satellite imagery

Knowledge Check

StyleGAN's generator starts from a learned constant, not from z. If z doesn't enter the generator directly, where does the variation come from?

Click to reveal

All variation comes from two sources: (1) w via AdaIN — injected at every layer, controlling style (shape, features, texture). (2) Per-layer noise injection — controlling random details (hair strand placement, pore patterns). The constant is just a starting canvas. z's influence enters indirectly through the mapping network: z → w → AdaIN.

In style mixing, you use w_A at coarse layers and w_B at fine layers. What would break if you tried this with a normal DCGAN?

Click to reveal

In DCGAN, z enters once at the very beginning and is transformed through the network. There's no mechanism to inject different control at different layers. z[0] affects everything — shape, color, texture — all entangled. StyleGAN's architecture makes style mixing possible because w is injected separately at each layer via AdaIN, and each layer has its own learned transformation from w to scale+shift.

You truncated z in L11. Why does StyleGAN truncate w instead?

Click to reveal

Both trade variety for quality. But z is entangled: shrinking it changes many features unevenly. The mapping network learns a W that is more disentangled and closer to linear, so moving w toward the average w̄ shrinks each style smoothly toward the average face — a cleaner, more predictable trade-off.
