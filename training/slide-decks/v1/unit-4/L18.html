Lesson 18

Data Augmentation & Privacy

GANs as a practical tool — synthetic data and differential privacy

The Data Hunger Problem

Medical Imaging

Rare diseases: maybe 50 X-rays exist worldwide. Each needs expert annotation. Privacy laws limit sharing.

Autonomous Driving

Need millions of miles of data. Rare events (accidents) are by definition uncommon. Can't crash cars on purpose.

Fraud Detection

99.9% of transactions are normal. Only 0.1% are fraud. New fraud types appear constantly.

Manufacturing

Defective products are rare (that's the point!). Need images of defects for quality control.

Traditional vs GAN Augmentation

Traditional: Transform Existing

  • RandomFlip, RandomRotation
  • RandomCrop, ColorJitter
  • Simple, fast, well-understood

Still the SAME images, just transformed.

Can't create truly new examples.

GAN: Generate New

  • Train GAN on real data
  • Generate synthetic images
  • Add to training set

COMPLETELY NEW images that look realistic.

More diversity, not just more copies.

Traditional: "Here's the same dog from a different angle"  vs  GAN: "Here's a completely new dog"

Does GAN Augmentation Actually Work?

Evidence: often, on small data

Liver lesions (CT)SensitivitySpecificity
Classic augmentation78.6%88.4%
+ GAN synthetic lesions85.7%92.4%

Frid-Adar et al., Neurocomputing 2018 · 182 lesions · see also DAGAN (Antoniou et al., 2017)

When it fails

  • Memorization: Dataset too small (<50), GAN just copies
  • No new information: a GAN can only remix what its training data contains
  • Mode collapse: Only a few variations, no diversity
  • GAN artifacts: Classifier learns artifacts instead of features

Check: FID, diversity, and accuracy on a real held-out test set the GAN never saw

The Privacy Risk: GANs Can Memorize

The Problem

A GAN trained on 10,000 faces might:

  • Generate a face that looks exactly like patient #4523
  • Memorize and reproduce rare individuals
  • Leak sensitive information

If someone recognizes "Mrs. Johnson" in synthetic data — privacy is violated.

Membership Inference Attack

Question: "Was this person's photo used to train the GAN?"

  • Generate many images from the GAN
  • Check if any are very similar to the target
  • Or: find a z that reproduces the target (GAN inversion)

If successful: privacy breach

Differential Privacy: The Gold Standard

The Guarantee

“Whether or not Mrs. Johnson's X-ray was in the training set, the GAN's outputs would be essentially the same.”

P(M(D) ∈ S) ≤ e^ε · P(M(D') ∈ S) + δ

D, D' = two datasets that differ by one person (here D is a dataset, not the discriminator) · δ ≈ 0, below 1/N

Privacy Budget ε

ε = 0Output cannot depend on any one person
ε = 1Strong: odds shift ≤ ×2.7
ε = 10Weak: odds shift ≤ ×22,026
ε = ∞No privacy (standard training)

DP-SGD: How to Train Privately

Normal SGD

grad = compute_gradient(batch)
weights -= lr * grad

DP-SGD (two changes)

g_i = grad(sample_i) # per sample
g_i = clip(g_i, max_norm=C)
g = (Σ g_i + N(0, σ²C²I)) / B
weights -= lr * g

Step 1: CLIP

Limits any single sample's influence.

Per sample: if ||g_i|| > C, scale it down to C (e.g. (3, 4) → (0.6, 0.8) for C = 1)

Step 2: ADD NOISE

Hides individual contributions.

Add N(0, σ²C²I) to the summed gradients. Apply to D only in a GAN.

Together

Clipping limits the signal. Noise hides it. No one can tell if a specific sample was used.

Why Better?

  • Privacy cost is paid only on noisy votes
  • Paper: better quality than DP-GAN at the same ε
  • Student D and G never touch real data

How It Works

Each teacher sees only 1/N of the data. Their votes are aggregated with noise. The student learns from noisy votes, not real data.

Practical Guidelines

Use GAN Augmentation When:

  • Small dataset (< 5000 images)
  • Class imbalance (minority < 10%)
  • Collecting more real data is expensive
  • You have enough data for a decent GAN (rule of thumb: >100)

Don't Use When:

  • Already large, balanced dataset
  • Fewer than ~50 images (GAN memorizes)
  • Exact photorealism required for safety
  • The GAN would be trained on your test data (leakage)

Checklist Before Sharing Synthetic Data

  • Trained with DP-SGD or PATE-GAN
  • Chosen reasonable ε (typically 1-10)
  • Verified quality (FID, visual inspection)
  • Checked for memorization (nearest-neighbor)
  • Validated downstream task performance
  • Documented privacy guarantees
  • Privacy team review

Knowledge Check

A GAN trained on 40 X-rays generates 1000 synthetic images. Will this help train a classifier?

Click to reveal

Probably not. With only 40 images, the GAN is likely to memorize the training data. The 1000 synthetic images would be near-copies of the 40 originals, adding quantity but not diversity. As a rule of thumb you need 100+ images before a GAN learns patterns rather than copies — and check with nearest-neighbour search.

A hospital uses a GAN to generate "anonymous" patient images without DP-SGD. Is this safe?

Click to reveal

No! Without differential privacy, the GAN may have memorized specific patients. A membership inference attack could reveal who was in the training data. "Synthetic" does not mean "private" unless you have mathematical guarantees (DP-SGD with a reasonable ε).

DP-SGD clips gradients AND adds noise. Why isn't clipping alone enough for privacy?

Click to reveal

Clipping limits how much any one sample can influence the gradient, but the direction of the influence is still visible. An attacker could still detect whether a specific sample was present by examining gradient directions. Noise is needed to hide the remaining signal, making it impossible to distinguish any individual's contribution.
