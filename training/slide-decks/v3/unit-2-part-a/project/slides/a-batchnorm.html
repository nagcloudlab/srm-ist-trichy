Part A · §36 · BatchNorm note

Be careful with BatchNorm in a WGAN-GP critic

Why

The gradient penalty measures gradients per input. BatchNorm mixes information across the batch.

Common alternatives

No normalisation in the critic, LayerNorm, or other per-sample choices.

BatchNorm makes each sample’s score depend on the other samples in the batch, which interferes with per-sample gradients. lab-09 in your repo uses InstanceNorm, another per-sample choice.
