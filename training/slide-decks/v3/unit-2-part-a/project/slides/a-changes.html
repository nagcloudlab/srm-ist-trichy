A one-slide map before the code. Each row is one change students will see in the lab notebook. The last layer loses its sigmoid. BCE is replaced by mean scores plus the gradient penalty. The generator maximises the critic score of its fakes. The critic gets five updates per generator update. No BatchNorm in the critic, because the penalty is computed per sample. Optimiser settings from Gulrajani et al. (2017): Adam with learning rate 1e-4 and betas (0, 0.9); classic DCGAN used 2e-4 and (0.5, 0.999).
