The whole of Part A in five steps. Start from the problem: the classic loss only says “wrong”. Each step fixes the one before: Wasserstein measures how far; a critic computes it with raw scores; the critic must not exaggerate (1-Lipschitz); clipping enforces that crudely; the gradient penalty does it properly.
