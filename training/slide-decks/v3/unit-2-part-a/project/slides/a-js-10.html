The problem

10 → 5 → 2 → 1 is real progress. JS stays at log 2 ≈ 0.693 the whole way.

Reveal one row at a time and ask the class each time: what is JS now? The real data sits at 0, the fake at 10, then 5, 2, 1. The two never overlap, so JS is exactly log 2 every time: the distance does not enter the formula at all. The JS slide in chapter 6 showed why it is exactly log 2: with no overlap the distance never enters the formula. G is clearly improving, but the number G is minimising does not move.
