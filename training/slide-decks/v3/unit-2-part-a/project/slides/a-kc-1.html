Can you answer these?

  1. Why does the original minimax generator loss saturate?
  2. Why does non-saturating BCE help?
  3. Why can JS stay at 0.693?
  4. Why is Wasserstein useful?
  5. Why use a critic instead of a probability discriminator?
Answers. Q1: Its gradient on the logit is −D(fake), near 0 when D rejects fakes. Q2: Its gradient is D(fake) − 1, near −1 when G is worst. Q3: With no overlap, JS is always log 2, whatever the distance. Q4: It measures how far, so it shrinks as G improves. Q5: Raw scores can grow with distance; probabilities saturate.
