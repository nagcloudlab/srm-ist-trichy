Part A · §40 · Suggested lab flow

Three labs

Lab A · Classic GAN vs WGAN

Observe loss behaviour, sample quality and training stability.

Lab B · Weight clipping

Try [−0.001, 0.001], [−0.01, 0.01] and [−0.1, 0.1]. Observe the critic.

Lab C · WGAN-GP

Track critic loss, generator loss, gradient penalty, mean gradient norm and samples.

Goal of the labs: see why the gradient penalty is more stable than simple clipping. In your repo, lab-08 covers A and B (WGAN with clipping, including c = 0.1 and 0.001), and lab-09 covers C.
