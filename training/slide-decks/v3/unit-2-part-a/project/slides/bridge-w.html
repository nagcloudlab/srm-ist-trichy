That “how far” number is the Wasserstein distance. Train a GAN to shrink it: a WGAN.

The bridge to chapter 8, on the same picture. Reveal one row at a time. Each row moves the fake pile closer: 10, 5, 2, 1, then right on top. The blue arrow is how far G still has to go. JS says 0.693 every time and only drops to 0 at the very end. What we want simply equals the length of the arrow: 10, 5, 2, 1, 0. That number has a name, the Wasserstein or earth mover’s distance, and training a GAN to shrink it gives a WGAN. Chapter 8 shows how to compute it by moving earth.
