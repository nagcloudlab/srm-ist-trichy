Unsure D: D(fake) is about 0.5, so the non-saturating push is 0.5, and D's score still has a real slope around the fake, 0.41: G feels about 0.21. Near-perfect D: the push is almost 1, bigger, but D's score is flat under the fakes, slope about 0.0005 (next slide shows this landscape): G feels about 0.0005, roughly 400 times less. The non-saturating loss fixes only the first factor. The rest of Part A is about the middle one.
