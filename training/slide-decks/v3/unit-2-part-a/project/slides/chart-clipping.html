Most weights stuck at ±c means a much simpler critic than the network could be.

A sketch of a finding reported in the WGAN-GP paper, Gulrajani et al. 2017: with weight clipping, most critic weights end up pressed against the two clipping limits, so the network behaves like a much simpler function and models only crude features. With the gradient penalty, weights spread out normally. The exact shapes here are illustrative; the point is the two spikes at the walls.
