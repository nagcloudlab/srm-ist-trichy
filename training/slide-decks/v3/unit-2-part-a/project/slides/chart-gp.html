Part A · §28 · Why square?

The penalty is a bowl centred at 1

━━ 10 · (norm − 1)² · x-axis: gradient norm, 0 → 3

norm 0 → 10 · norm 1 → 0 · norm 2 → 10 · norm 3 → 40

The penalty as a picture. Zero only at norm exactly 1, the bottom of the bowl. It grows on both sides, so a critic that is too flat is pushed to become steeper and one that is too steep is pushed to become flatter. Because the bowl is squared, being far off costs a lot: norm 3 costs 40, four times norm 2. Lambda = 10 sets how steep the walls are.
