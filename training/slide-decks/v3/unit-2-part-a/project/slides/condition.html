Part A · §18 · The rule: 1-Lipschitz

The rule: a speed limit on the score

Move the input by 1 step → the score may move by at most 1.

|C(x₁) − C(x₂)| ≤ |x₁ − x₂|

C = −x

input moves 1 → score moves 1

allowed ✓

C = −0.5x

input moves 1 → score moves 0.5

allowed ✓

C = −5x

input moves 1 → score moves 5

too fast ✗

Name

A critic that obeys this speed limit is called 1-Lipschitz.

The phrase 1-Lipschitz sounds scary; the idea is a speed limit. Moving the input by one step may move the score by at most one. The formula says the same thing for any two inputs. Check the three critics from before: slope 1 is right at the limit, allowed; slope 0.5 is under it, allowed; slope 5 moves the score five times faster than the input, not allowed.
