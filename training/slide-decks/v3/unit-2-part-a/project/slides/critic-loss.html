Same picture and the same four critics as the dual-form slide. The critic loss is mean fake score minus mean real score. With real at 0 and fake at 10: the flat critic gives 0; slope 0.5 gives −5; slope 1 gives −10; slope 5 would give −50 but breaks the slope rule. The critic minimises its loss, so it ends up at −10, which is minus the Wasserstein distance. In code: critic_loss = fake_score.mean() − real_score.mean().
