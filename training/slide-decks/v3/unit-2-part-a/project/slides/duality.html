Why it helps

No transport plans. Searching for a function is what a neural network does.

This is the key move of WGAN, made concrete on our picture. The dual form says: instead of searching transport plans, search for a scoring function f that gives real data a high score and fake data a low score, but with a slope of at most 1. Try four candidates with real at 0 and fake at 10. Flat f gives gap 0. Slope 0.5 gives gap 5. Slope 1 gives gap 10. Slope 5 would give 50, but it breaks the slope rule, so it is not allowed. The best allowed gap is 10, exactly the distance between the piles: W. In WGAN, f is a neural network called the critic, and training it to maximise the gap computes W. Chapter 10 explains the slope rule.
