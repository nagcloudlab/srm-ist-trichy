Recall · Unit 1

Each batch: one D step, then one G step, and repeat over many epochs. Neither network ever "finishes"; each keeps adapting to the other. This alternating structure is why GAN loss curves look so different from normal classifiers: the target keeps moving. Remember the ratio, 1 to 1; WGAN, in chapter 12, changes it to 5 to 1 and explains why.
