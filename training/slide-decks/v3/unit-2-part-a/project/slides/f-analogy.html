Part A · Recall · The GAN game

A forger versus a detective

Generator

Forger

Paints fakes. Learns only from how the detective catches them.

Discriminator

Detective

Sees real and fake paintings. Learns to spot the difference.

Both improve until the fakes can’t be told apart.

Recall · Unit 1

The forger and detective from Unit 1, L01. Stress the detail students forget: the forger never studies the real paintings. It only learns from the detective’s reaction, which in a GAN is D’s gradient. If that feedback is useless, the forger cannot improve, and that is what Unit 2 is about.
