Part A · Recall · D’s loss

D’s loss: BCE on real + BCE on fake

L_D =

−log D(real)

real image, target 1

+

−log(1 − D(fake))

fake image, target 0

Good D: right on both

−log 0.9 = 0.11

−log(1 − 0.2) = 0.22

L_D = 0.33

Fooled D: wrong on both

−log 0.3 = 1.20

−log(1 − 0.8) = 1.61

L_D = 2.81

D wants real → 1 and fake → 0. Then both terms shrink.

Recall · Unit 1

The discriminator's loss is just BCE twice: real images with target 1 give minus log D(real); fake images with target 0 give minus log of 1 minus D(fake). On each scale, see where D puts the two images. A good D puts real near 1 and fake near 0: loss 0.11 + 0.22 = 0.33. A fooled D has them the wrong way round: 1.20 + 1.61 = 2.81. The bars show how much each term contributes, blue for real and orange for fake. Nothing GAN-specific yet: this is a normal classifier.
