Part A · Recall · Training D

One discriminator step

1

Real batch → D, target 1

d_real = D(x)

2

Fake, detached → D, target 0

d_fake = D(G(z).detach())

3

Add the two BCEs

loss_D = bce(d_real, 1) + bce(d_fake, 0)

4

Backward, step D only

loss_D.backward(); opt_D.step()

Result: D gets a little better at telling real from fake.

Recall · Unit 1

Walk the steps slowly, pointing at the diagram. Step 2 is the subtle one: we detach the fake images because in this step G is not learning; we only want D to learn to label them 0. In the diagram the gradient (dashed) updates D and is cut at the scissors, so nothing reaches G. Without detach, backprop would also compute gradients for G, wasting time. Remember opt_D.zero_grad() before the backward.
