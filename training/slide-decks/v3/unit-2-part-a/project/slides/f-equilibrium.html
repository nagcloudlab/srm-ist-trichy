Recall · Unit 1

Where should the tug of war end? Read it as a chain. One: G's cloud sits exactly on the real cloud, p_g = p_r. Two: so D cannot do better than a coin flip, 0.5 for every image. Three: so V = log 0.5 + log 0.5 = −log 4, about −1.39. Remember this number: −log 4 comes back when JS appears. At that point neither player can improve by changing only itself: a Nash equilibrium. Goodfellow et al. proved this is the global optimum of the minimax game. In practice, with finite networks and alternating updates, GANs circle around it or never quite reach it, which is part of why training is unstable.
