Part A · Recall · Maths warm-up

E[ … ] just means “average over a batch”

E[log D(real)] ≈ (1/N) · Σ log D(xᵢ)

Batch of 4

log 0.9 = −0.11

log 0.8 = −0.22

log 0.95 = −0.05

log 0.7 = −0.36

Average = −0.18. In PyTorch that is simply .mean().

Recall · Unit 1

Many students freeze at the E symbol. E means expected value, and in practice we estimate it with an average over a mini-batch. Example: D gives four real images the probabilities 0.9, 0.8, 0.95, 0.7. Their natural logs are about -0.11, -0.22, -0.05, -0.36. The average is about -0.18. Every E[...] in this unit becomes a .mean() in code. Also: log here always means the natural log, ln.
