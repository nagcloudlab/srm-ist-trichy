2

loss_G.backward()

opt_G.step()

G never sees a real image. Its only teacher is the gradient from D.

Recall · Unit 1

Key idea: G never sees a real image. Its only teacher is D's gradient. In step 3 the error signal travels backward through D's layers, which stay fixed because we only step G's optimizer, and then into G's layers. This is why the quality of D's gradient decides everything, and it is exactly what the rest of this deck questions. D also receives gradients in this step; they are thrown away by opt_D.zero_grad() before D's next update.
