The saturation story starts with G's goal, in one line: push D(fake) up, from "D sees right through it" toward "G wins". G cannot change real images, so of the two halves of the scoreboard it only affects the fake half. The original 2014 loss for G is exactly that half, log of 1 minus D(fake), and G tries to make it small. The goal is fine. The question for the next slides is: how hard does this loss push G, especially at the start?
