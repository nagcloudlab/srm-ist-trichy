Recall · Unit 1

Say: two neural networks. The generator takes random noise z and turns it into an image. The discriminator sees an image, either real from the dataset or fake from G, and outputs one number between 0 and 1: its belief that the image is real. D is trained like any binary classifier. G is trained to make D say "real" for its fakes. Everything in this unit is about which loss we use to train these two.
