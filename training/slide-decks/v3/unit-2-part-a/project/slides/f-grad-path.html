Recall · Unit 1

Draw this on the board too. Forward pass, left to right: noise, generator, image, discriminator, logit, sigmoid, loss. Backward pass, right to left, multiplies three factors. The first, how the loss reacts to D's score, is what the non-saturating loss fixes. The second, D's slope around the fake, is what the critic, Lipschitz and the gradient penalty fix. The third is G's own network. D's weights are not updated in G's step, but D's shape decides the direction G moves. The chain-rule slide later is this same picture as an equation.
