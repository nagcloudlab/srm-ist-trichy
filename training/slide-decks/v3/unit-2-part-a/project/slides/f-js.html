JS: compare both to their average

JS = ½ KL(real ‖ m) + ½ KL(fake ‖ m),   m = average of the two

KL(real ‖ m) = log(1 / 0.5)

= log 2

KL(fake ‖ m) = log(1 / 0.5)

= log 2

JS = ½ log 2 + ½ log 2

≈ 0.693

never ∞: m covers both piles

0 when identical

max log 2: no overlap

JS fixes KL by comparing each pile to their average m instead of to each other. In our picture m is half a pile at 0 and half a pile at 10, so m is never zero where either pile lives and nothing can blow up. Each pile sees m as exactly half of itself, so each KL is log 2, and JS is log 2, about 0.693. JS is symmetric, 0 when the piles coincide, and at most log 2, reached when they do not overlap at all. Remember 0.693: it is about to cause trouble.
