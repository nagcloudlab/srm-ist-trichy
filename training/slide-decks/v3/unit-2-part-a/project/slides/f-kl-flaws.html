Part A · New tool · KL’s two habits

KL has two awkward habits

1 · Not symmetric

KL(p ‖ q)

0.51

KL(q ‖ p)

0.37

Same two distributions, two answers. Which one is “the” distance?

2 · Can explode

cats 1.0

dogs 0

dogs: 0.5 · log(0.5 / 0) = ∞

G makes no dogs at all: one missing outcome breaks KL.

Habit one: KL(p‖q) = 0.51 but KL(q‖p) = 0.37, using the same cats and dogs. A distance should not depend on which side you start from. Habit two: if the generator makes no dogs at all, q(dogs) = 0, and the dogs term becomes 0.5 · log(0.5 / 0), which is infinite. In GANs, the fake data often misses whole regions of the real data, so this is not rare. JS, next, fixes both habits.
