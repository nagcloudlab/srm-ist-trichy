Recall · Unit 1

This slide removes most confusion about GAN code. D step: real images get target 1, fakes get target 0, and only D's optimizer steps. The fake is detached so no gradient reaches G. G step: the same kind of fake now gets target 1, because G wants D to call it real. The gradient passes through D, but only G's optimizer steps. The losses on the right are the BCE terms that survive, from the BCE slide. The G-step loss −log D(G(z)) is the non-saturating loss we meet properly later.
