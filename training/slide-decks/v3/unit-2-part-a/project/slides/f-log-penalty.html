Right and confident: almost no loss. Unsure, p = 0.5: loss 0.69, which is log 2. Confidently wrong: the loss shoots up, and goes to infinity as p goes to 0. Keep the number 0.69 = log 2 in mind; it comes back when we meet JS divergence.
