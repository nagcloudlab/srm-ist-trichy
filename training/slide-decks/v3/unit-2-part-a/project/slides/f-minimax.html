min_G max_D V

max_D

D turns the score up

min_G

G turns the score down

Mini + max = minimax: two players, one number, opposite goals.

Recall · Unit 1

Only now show the formal notation, after the class already understands the game. The formula is just a label for the scoreboard picture: max over D means D tries to turn V up; min over G means G tries to turn V down. Read it right to left if asked: imagine D playing as well as it can, and G choosing itself to make even that best D score as low as possible. If a student asks why it is called minimax, it is borrowed from game theory, where the same name is used for chess-playing programs that minimise the opponent's best outcome.
