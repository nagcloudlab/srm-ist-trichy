Part A · §4 · Saturation · The name

“Saturating” = stuck on a flat part

Flat tails: slope ≈ 0, so almost no gradient gets through.

Early on, D rejects fakes easily, so fakes sit in the left tail. G learns almost nothing.

This is the sigmoid σ(a), plotted exactly. a is D's raw score, the logit, before the sigmoid. In the middle, at a = 0, the slope is 0.25: the dashed tangent. Beyond about ±4 the curve is flat, shaded orange: the slope is nearly zero, so backpropagation passes almost no signal through. That is what "saturated" means, like a sponge that cannot absorb more water. Early in training D spots fakes easily: a fake might get a = −6.9, so D(fake) ≈ 0.001, and the slope there is also about 0.001. That is exactly the push from the original loss on the previous slide.
