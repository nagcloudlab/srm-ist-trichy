Recall · Unit 1

The discriminator's network ends with one number, any real value: the logit a. The sigmoid turns it into a probability. Big positive logit: close to 1, real. Big negative: close to 0, fake. Zero: 0.5, undecided. Keep this picture: almost every gradient we compute later is with respect to a, the score before the sigmoid.
