The scoreboard in action. Start: D gives real images 0.9 and fakes 0.1, so V = log 0.9 + log 0.9, about −0.21. D trains and gets sharper on both: V rises to about −0.10, closer to the perfect 0. Then G trains: its fakes fool D a bit more, D(fake) goes up to 0.3, the second half becomes log 0.7, and V drops to about −0.41. In the third card D(real) is greyed out: it did not move, because G has no way to touch real images. The dashed line shows the zig-zag continuing.
