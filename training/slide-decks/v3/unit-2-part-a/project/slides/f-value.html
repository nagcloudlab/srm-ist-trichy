Recall · Unit 1

Now write the score down, in two halves. E means the average over a batch. Real half: how sure is D that real images are real? log D(x): 0 when D says 1, more negative the more wrong D is. Fake half: how sure is D that fakes are fake? log(1 − D(G(z))), same idea mirrored. The tables are the BCE numbers with the sign flipped: V is D's BCE loss with its minus sign removed, so D maximising V is the same as D minimising its loss. Check with the scoreboard: the sharp D had −0.11 + −0.22 = −0.33.
