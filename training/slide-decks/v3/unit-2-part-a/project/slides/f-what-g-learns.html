Recall · Unit 1

An important mental shift. G is not trying to copy one image. Each random z gives a different output, so G defines a whole cloud of images, a probability distribution p_g. The real dataset is another cloud, p_r. Reveal the three panels in turn: far apart, closer, matched in both position and spread. Keep this picture: chapters 6 to 8 ask how to measure the gap between the clouds, and the answer will be JS, then Wasserstein.
