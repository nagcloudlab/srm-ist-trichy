D(fake) ≈ 0

So the region D(fake) near 0 is where every GAN begins. Remember it.

This slide sets up saturation. At initialisation, G produces garbage. Telling garbage from real images is trivial, so D quickly becomes confident: D(fake) values of 0.01 or less are common early on. Whatever loss we give G must therefore work well when D(fake) is near 0. The next slides show that the original minimax loss does not.
