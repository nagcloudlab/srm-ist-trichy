Five equations worth remembering

Non-saturating G loss

L_G = −log D(G(z))

JS on disjoint data

JS = log 2 ≈ 0.693

W for two points

W = |x_real − x_fake|

WGAN losses

L_C = E[C(fake)] − E[C(real)] · L_G = −E[C(fake)]

Gradient penalty

GP = λ(‖∇C(x̂)‖₂ − 1)²

If students remember only five lines of math from this unit, these are the ones.
