JS = ½ log 2 + ½ log 2

= log 2

Move the fake from 10 to 1: every number above stays the same. The distance never appears.

With no overlap, the average m puts half its mass on the real point and half on the fake point. Each side sees m as exactly half of itself, so each KL is log(1/0.5) = log 2, and their average is log 2. Nothing in this calculation mentions where the fake is, only that it does not overlap the real. That is the whole JS problem in three lines.
