Same journey, two very different signals

━━ JS · ━━ W · (left = fake on top of real, right = far away)

W has a slope everywhere — a gradient that points home.

For one-point distributions, W equals the distance between them, so it is a straight line, continuous and differentiable almost everywhere. JS is a step. This is Example 1 in the WGAN paper by Arjovsky, Chintala and Bottou, 2017, with parallel lines.
