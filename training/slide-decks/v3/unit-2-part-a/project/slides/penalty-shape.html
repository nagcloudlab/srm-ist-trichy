Part A · §27 · Why subtract 1, and why square?

Too steep or too flat: both get penalised

− 1 sets the target: zero when the norm is exactly 1.

Squaring makes too-low and too-high both cost something.

Note this is a two-sided penalty: it also pushes gradients up toward 1 if the critic is too flat. Strictly, Lipschitz only needs the norm to be at most 1, and a one-sided version exists, but the paper found the two-sided form works well because the optimal critic has norm 1 on those lines anyway.
