Formally: the cheapest way to move the earth

E[ ‖x − y‖ ]

average distance each bit of earth moves

■ real: half at 0, half at 6  ·  ■ fake: half at 4, half at 10  ·  a plan says which fake earth goes to which real spot

Plan A: move to the nearest

cost = ½·4 + ½·4 = 4 ✓ cheapest

Plan B: cross over

cost = ½·2 + ½·10 = 6

But

On a line, trying every plan is easy. For images, with millions of dimensions, it is hopeless. Chapter 9: a trick.

The formal definition in plain words: over every possible transport plan, take the cheapest average distance moved. Make it concrete. Real data: half a pile at 0, half at 6. Fake data: half at 4, half at 10. Plan A moves 4 to 0 and 10 to 6: each half travels 4, so the cost is 4. Plan B crosses over: 4 to 6 travels 2, 10 to 0 travels 10, average 6. W picks the cheapest, so W = 4. On a line we can list the plans; for 64×64 colour images, 12,288 dimensions and millions of points, solving this every training step is far too expensive. Chapter 9 gives the trick: the dual form.
