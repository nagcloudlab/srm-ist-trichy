One game

The critic measures the distance; the generator shrinks it.

One picture for both WGAN losses, with the running numbers. Take a small batch: real samples near 0 score about 1, 0, −1 under the critic C(x) = −x, mean 0; fakes near 10 score about −9, −10, −11, mean −10. Every image gets a score on one line. The critic pulls reals right and fakes left, widening the bracket, but only as far as the slope rule allows: here the bracket is 10, which is W. Its loss is mean fake minus mean real, −10. The generator only moves the orange dots: it slides its fakes right, toward the reals; its loss is minus the mean fake score, 10, and it shrinks as the fakes move.
