In images

Many inputs, so the slope is the length of the gradient arrow. Keep it ≤ 1: the gradient penalty does exactly this.

For a smooth function in 1D, 1-Lipschitz is the same as the absolute derivative being at most 1 everywhere. In many dimensions, it is the gradient norm being at most 1. That is the form WGAN-GP will use. One-line summary: keep the critic slope controlled.
