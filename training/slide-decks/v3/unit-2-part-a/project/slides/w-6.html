Move the pile closer: W shrinks every time

Same picture. Each time G moves its fake pile closer, the earth has a shorter trip home.

fake at 10

1 × 10 steps

W = 10

fake at 5

1 × 5 steps

W = 5

fake at 2

1 × 2 steps

W = 2

fake at 0

1 × 0 steps

W = 0

Contrast

JS said 0.693, 0.693, 0.693. W says 10, 5, 2, 0: it measures progress.

Chapter 8 with the running picture. The fake pile has one unit of mass. Moving it home costs mass times steps: 10, then 5, then 2, then 0 when it lands on the real pile. Put this next to the JS slide: there the number was frozen at 0.693 until the very end; here it shrinks with every step G takes.
