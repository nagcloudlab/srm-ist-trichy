Why does G want the score down?

The logic

  1. V is D’s report card: high = D tells real from fake.
  2. G wins by fooling D on the fakes.
  3. D fooled → report card worse → V falls.

Key idea

V is D’s score. G’s job is to make D look bad, so G pushes it down.

Recall · Unit 1

Analogy from Unit 1: V is the detective’s catch rate. The detective wants it high; the forger wants it low. A low catch rate is what winning looks like for the forger. The fake half of V is log(1 − D(fake)): as G improves, D(fake) rises and this falls.
