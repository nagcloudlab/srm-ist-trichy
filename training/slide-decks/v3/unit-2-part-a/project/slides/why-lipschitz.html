Part A · §18 · Why a speed limit?

Without a rule, the critic can cheat

Real at 0, fake at 10. The critic wants the biggest gap. Nothing stops it from making its line steeper:

slope 1

10

= the real distance ✓

50

1 000

∞

training explodes

Like a teacher who can give 1000 marks out of 100: the marks stop meaning anything.

The fix

Cap the slope at 1. Then the biggest gap is 10: the true distance.

Start chapter 10 with the motivation. With real at 0 and fake at 10, a critic with slope k scores them 10k apart. If nothing limits k, the critic simply makes its line steeper: gap 50, 1000, infinity. The number no longer measures distance, and training blows up. The fix is a speed limit on the slope: at most 1. Then the largest possible gap is exactly 10, the Wasserstein distance. That limit has a name: 1-Lipschitz.
