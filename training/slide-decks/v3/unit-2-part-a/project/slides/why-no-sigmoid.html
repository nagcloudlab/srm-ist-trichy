Why drop the sigmoid? A 0–1 ruler can’t measure 10

A sigmoid squeezes every answer into 0 … 1. Like measuring a road trip with a ruler that stops at 1 metre.

Key idea

A probability can only say “fake”. A raw score can say “how far”.

Two probabilities can differ by at most 1, so after a sigmoid the gap between real and fake scores is stuck below 1 whether the piles are 1, 10 or 100 apart; it also saturates, as in the recall chapters. A raw score has no ceiling: the best slope-1 critic gives a gap of exactly the distance, 1, 10 or 100. That is why the critic has no sigmoid.
