Analogy

y is the order slip you hand the chef: “one 7, please”.

For MNIST, y is the digit class, 0 to 9. The condition is simply a second input next to the noise. Keep the chef analogy: the noise is still there, but now there is an order slip too. This model is the Conditional GAN, cGAN.
