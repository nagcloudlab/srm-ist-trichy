Part B · §24 · Latent space

z is not “just random numbers”: it’s a location

z = [0.2, −1.3, 0.7, …]

A point in the generator’s input space: the latent space.

Analogy

z is an address on a map. G is the city: give it an address, it shows you the house there.

Shift the mental picture: each z is a place, and the generator turns places into images.
