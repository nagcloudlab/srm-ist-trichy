Part B · §47 · Phrases to remember

Six phrases to carry out of the room

Normal GAN

“Generate something.”

Conditional GAN

“Generate THIS.”

Controllable generation

“Generate this, LIKE THIS.”

Latent space

“Where generation choices live.”

Latent direction

“Move this way to change an attribute.”

Disentanglement

“Change one thing without changing everything else.”

Say them aloud; have the class repeat them.
