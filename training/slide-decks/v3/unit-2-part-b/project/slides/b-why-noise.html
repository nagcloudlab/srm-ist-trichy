Part B · §6 · Why still use noise?

The label says WHAT. The noise says HOW.

label = identity / class

Which digit: always a 7

noise = diversity

Which 7: thin, thick, tilted, slanted, wide

If the label already says 7, why keep the noise? Because the label fixes the category and the noise gives variation within it. Fonts stand in for handwriting here.
