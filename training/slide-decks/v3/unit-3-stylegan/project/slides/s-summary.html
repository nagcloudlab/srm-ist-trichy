StyleGAN · §12 · Summary

StyleGAN in eight lines

1

Problem

In DCGAN one z controls everything: entangled.

2

Mapping network

z → 8 FC → w: a less tangled style space.

3

Learned constant

The image starts from a fixed learned 4×4 canvas.

4

AdaIN

Wash (normalise), then paint (γ, β from w) at every layer.

5

Layer levels

Coarse: pose, shape · medium: features · fine: colour, texture.

6

Style mixing

Different w per layer: A’s shape, B’s colour.

7

Noise

Random per-pixel detail: where each hair falls.

8

Truncation

w_mean + ψ(w − w_mean): quality vs variety.

Read the chain aloud, or ask a student to tell it. Line 1 is the problem; every other line is one of StyleGAN’s answers.
