A qualitative comparison, no maths. GANs give sharp images in a single fast pass, which is why they are still used for real-time and image-to-image work. Their weaknesses are exactly this unit’s topics: fragile training, mode collapse, and no built-in score, which is why we need FID. VAEs are stable but blurrier; diffusion models are high quality but slow; autoregressive models are slow but measurable.
