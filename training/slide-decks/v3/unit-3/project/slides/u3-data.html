Unit 3 · §8 · Data bias

The GAN learns what it is shown

CelebA, the best-known face dataset

About 77% of faces labelled “young”; mostly light-skinned, posed celebrity photos.

What a GAN learns from it

Older and darker-skinned faces are rarer, and come out blurrier.

CelebA, the most-used face dataset, is about 77% labelled young (and about 58% labelled female), mostly light-skinned, and made of posed celebrity photos. A GAN trained on it produces a “default face” to match, and the rarer faces get less training signal, so they are lower quality.
