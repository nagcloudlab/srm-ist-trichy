Unit 3 · §5 · The pipeline

FID in five steps

1 · Collect

Thousands of real images, and the same number of generated ones.

2 · Prepare

Resize every image the same way (299 × 299 for Inception).

3 · Describe

Run each through Inception v3 and keep its 2,048 features.

4 · Summarise

Find each crowd’s centre and spread.

5 · Compare

How far apart are the crowds? That is the FID.

The recipe, in words. In lab-13 students build each step from scratch; in practice the pytorch-fid package does it in one command. The important rule hides in step 2: real and fake images must be prepared identically.
