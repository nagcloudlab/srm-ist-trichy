Quick check: judging GANs

a

A GAN draws one perfect face, again and again. Quality? Variety?

two words

Quality high, variety zero: mode collapse.

b

Why can’t the Inception Score catch “we trained on cats, generated dogs”?

what does it look at?

It never looks at the real data.

c

FID: 35 for model A, 18 for model B (same set-up). Which is better?

direction

B: lower FID is better.

d

Real vs real with only 100 images gives FID ≈ 21. Why not 0?

sample count

Too few images bias FID upward. Use 10,000+.

Reveal one answer at a time. If students get c wrong, revisit the direction: lower is better for FID.
