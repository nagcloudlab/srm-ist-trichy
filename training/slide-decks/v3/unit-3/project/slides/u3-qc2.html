Quick check: bias

a

Data is 70% A, 30% B. Output is 85% A. Name it.

more or less skewed?

Amplification: the GAN made the imbalance worse.

b

A face GAN collapses to five face types. Who disappears first?

which modes survive?

Under-represented groups.

c

Overall FID is 22. Should we ship?

what can an average hide?

Not yet: check per-group FID first.

d

After rebalancing, FID rose from 22 to 27. Failure?

what does FID measure?

No: FID rewards copying the skew. A fairer model can score worse.

Reveal one at a time. c and d are the two habits to build: break scores down by group, and don’t treat FID as a fairness score.
