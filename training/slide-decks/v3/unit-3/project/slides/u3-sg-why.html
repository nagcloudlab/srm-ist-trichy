Unit 3 · §10 · Why it matters

Why StyleGAN set the standard

Photorealistic faces

The first GAN whose 1024 × 1024 faces fooled most people.

Separate controls

Pose, features and texture can be edited independently.

Cleaner directions

Part B’s latent directions work far better in its style space.

Kept improving

StyleGAN2 removed blob artifacts; StyleGAN3 stopped textures sticking to pixels.

Still has the bias problem

Trained on skewed faces, it reproduces the skew: audit it too.

Why StyleGAN matters. Realistic faces, independent controls, and a style space where editing directions work cleanly. Later versions fixed visual artifacts. And the last row ties the unit together: even the best GAN inherits its data’s bias, so every chapter today applies to it.
