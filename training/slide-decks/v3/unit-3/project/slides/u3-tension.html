Unit 3 · §9 · The trade-off

A fairer GAN can have a worse FID, and that’s fine

The data is 80 / 20

FID rewards copying that split exactly, bias included.

We rebalance to 50 / 50

The output no longer matches the skewed data, so FID goes up.

Which is better?

The fairer model. A higher FID here is a feature, not a bug.

Lesson

Metrics measure match to the data, not fairness. Decide what “good” means for your use.

An honest tension. FID measures how well the output matches the training data, including its skew. Rebalancing for fairness moves away from that skew, so FID rises. That is not failure. A score is a tool; deciding what counts as good depends on how the GAN will be used and who could be harmed.
