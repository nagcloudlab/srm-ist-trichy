The wider toolbox from L12 and L13. FID is the default. KID is a close cousin that behaves better with small sample sizes. Precision and recall separate quality from variety. LPIPS compares pairs of images the way people perceive them and is used in Units 4 and 5. The Inception Score is for reading older papers.
