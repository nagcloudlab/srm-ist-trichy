Unit 3 · §5 · Tracking training

Use FID to decide when to stop

Read it

Falling: learning. Flat: converged. Rising: something broke. Keep the best checkpoint: epoch 25.

An illustrative run from the L13 lesson. FID falls fast, flattens around epoch 25, then rises again, which signals collapse or overfitting. So we save checkpoints and keep the one with the lowest FID, not the last one. This is far more reliable than watching the losses.
